Gesture Fire

A first-person shooter with no keyboard, no mouse — every action is a hand gesture (or a head tilt) read from your webcam. Left hand: open palm walks forward, a fist stops you. Tilt your head left or right to turn. Right hand: a finger-gun aims, curling the trigger finger fires, and an open palm reloads.

Running in keyboard mock mode (?mock=1) — no camera needed. W to move, A/D to turn, hold Shift to aim, Space to fire, R to reload.
